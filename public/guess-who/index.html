    Actions

    I built this after trying to make a version for my daughter's birthday and finding a sea of paid Etsy templates plus tedious dragging and dropping. The point of this page is to skip all that manual layout work: pick some faces, tweak the names if you want, and get printable sheets for free.


    Details

    The board-card image area is roughly 8:7. Images do not need to match that exactly: transparent or white borders are cropped away first, then the image is scaled to fit, which can leave extra background around narrower or taller characters.

    This expects 24 images which is standard for Guess Who though it should work with any number.
